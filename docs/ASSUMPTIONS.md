# Assumptions

These assumptions frame the current build. They should be validated with Genii
before launch.

## Data & access

- **No internal data access.** No live Genii platform data, customer interaction
  data, CRM records or analytics back-end were connected during this build. All
  numbers, metrics and proof points come from the supplied positioning and design
  documents.
- **No production credentials.** Lead-form submission, analytics IDs and any
  integration keys are placeholders until Genii supplies them (see
  `TRACKING_PLAN.md` and `DEPLOYMENT.md`).

## Content & strategy

- **Directional strategy only.** Copy, structure and product positioning reflect
  the supplied briefs (homepage design brief + product-page positioning document)
  and the stated core positioning. They are a directional starting point, not
  legally or commercially signed-off final copy.
- **Metrics are illustrative.** Outcome figures (e.g. "30%+ conversion increase",
  "1B interactions analysed", "13+ years", "100+ brands") are taken directly from
  the briefs and must be confirmed as approved, defensible claims before launch.
- **Client logos & names** (MTN, Wave Mobile Inc., Old Mutual, TIH, Foodhub) are
  referenced per the brief. Accurate, licensed logo files and any required usage
  permissions must be supplied before go-live. Placeholders are used until then.
- **Brand assets.** The Genii logo is used from the current live website
  (`public/images/genii-logo.svg`). The logo must not be recreated, stretched or
  recoloured.

## Requires validation post-approval

- Final copy sign-off across all sections.
- Confirmed, defensible statistics and claims.
- Real client logos with usage rights.
- Lead-routing destination (email or CRM) and analytics IDs.
- The explainer video asset and thumbnail.
- Legal / privacy / cookie compliance review (POPIA / GDPR as applicable).
- The platform decision (new stack vs. WordPress update).
